# What Is Chain-of-Thought Reasoning?

Understanding how modern AI systems solve problems step-by-step instead of jumping directly to answers.

---

# 🧠 Overview

Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning is a prompting and reasoning technique where an AI model breaks a problem into intermediate reasoning steps before producing a final answer.

Instead of immediately generating an output, the model:

- thinks through the problem,
- explains intermediate steps,
- and gradually arrives at a conclusion.

The core idea is simple:

> Better reasoning often comes from thinking step-by-step.
