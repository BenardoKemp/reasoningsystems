# Reflection Loops in AI Systems

Understanding how modern AI systems improve reasoning by reviewing, critiquing, and refining their own outputs before producing final answers.

---

# 🧠 Overview

Reflection loops are iterative reasoning mechanisms where an AI system evaluates its own outputs, identifies potential mistakes, and refines its reasoning before returning a final response.

Instead of producing a single immediate answer, the system performs cycles of:

- reasoning,
- self-evaluation,
- correction,
- and refinement.

The central idea is:

> AI systems can improve performance by reflecting on their own reasoning.
